EU Regulatory Quality Index

Benchmarking EU legislation against regulatory quality standards · KEFiM · November 2025

A data-driven benchmark of how well EU directives are written and made. It pairs a statistical assessment of legal language and length with a composite index (EU-RQI) scoring directives from 2022 to 2024 on initiative, subsidiarity, drafting, impact assessment, consultation, and implementation, and closes with recommendations for EU lawmakers.

My role: junior researcher, working on directive-level evaluation and Excel-based data tracking.
